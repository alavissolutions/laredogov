# 15: Ask Webb County for feed access

**What to build:** A written request to Webb County's IT or public information office asking for RSS or API access to Commissioners Court agendas and News Flash, or an allowlist for the site's fetcher, explaining the project and its polite fetching. The answer, or the absence of one after a month, decides whether phase two adds an email-ingest Feed.
