# Only government-published Sources; social-media-only content is Directory-only

Feeds ingest only what a Publisher posts on its own web property or an official platform it controls (Legistar, CivicPlus, ArcGIS Hub, GovDelivery). Local news media (KGNS, Laredo Morning Times) are not ingested even where an open RSS feed exists. Publishers that post only on Facebook or X (Fire Department monthly stats, Traffic Safety closures, TxDOT Laredo advisories) get a Directory entry that says so, and nothing is fetched from those platforms.

## Why

The site's claim is "what the government publishes, in one place." Mixing in news coverage blurs that claim and adds copyright exposure. Scraping Facebook or X violates their terms and breaks constantly. Saying plainly that a Publisher only posts on Facebook is itself useful to residents and a nudge to the Publisher.
